Every budget optimization deliverable must include these elements:

**1. Current vs. recommended allocation table:**
```
Channel       | Current ($) | Recommended ($) | Change    | Expected Lift
--------------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|---------------
Paid Search   | $2.5M       | $3.2M           | +28%      | +$1.8M [+$1.1M–+$2.5M 90% HDI]
Social Video  | $3.0M       | $2.1M           | -30%      |
TV            | $4.5M       | $4.7M           | +4%       |
Total         | $10M        | $10M            | 0%        | +$1.8M [+$1.1M–+$2.5M]
```

**2. Marginal ROAS at current and recommended:**
```
Channel       | Current mROAS | Recommended mROAS | Status
--------------|---------------|-------------------|------------------
Paid Search   | 4.1x          | 2.8x              | Under-invested → increase
Social Video  | 0.6x          | 1.2x              | Over-invested → decrease
```

**3. Extrapolation cap statement (mandatory):**
> "Budget optimisation is capped at 2× observed spend per channel. Recommendations beyond this range rely on response curve extrapolation and carry higher uncertainty."

**4. Sensitivity table (±10%, ±20% total budget):**
```
Budget      | Expected KPI | vs. Current
$8M (-20%)  | $X           | -Y%
$9M (-10%)  | $X           | -Y%
$10M (base) | $X           | —
$11M (+10%) | $X           | +Y%
$12M (+20%) | $X           | +Y%
```
