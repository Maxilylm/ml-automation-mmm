id: CAL_003
title: When NOT to calibrate — exclusion criteria
source: pymc_marketing/mmm/lift_test.py
category: calibration
tags: [calibration, exclusion, lift_test, pitfall, data_quality]
---

Do not apply lift test calibration when any of the following apply:

1. **Anomalous experiment period:** Test ran during holiday, COVID lockdown, concurrent major promo, or any period not representative of normal operations.

2. **Low market coverage:** Experiment geos represent < 10% of total modelled sales volume. Small holdout regions cannot reliably estimate market-level iROAS.

3. **Co-active channels in same arm:** Multiple channels changed spend simultaneously in the treatment arm. The lift is not attributable to the target channel alone.

4. **Extreme iROAS vs. prior range:** Measured iROAS is > 5× the model's prior range. This usually signals a data quality issue in the experiment or the model data — investigate before calibrating.

5. **Experiment targeting mismatch:** The channel in the model is a spend column but the experiment measured visits/impressions impact. Units must match.

**What to do instead:**
- Flag the channel as "uncalibrated" in the deliverable.
- Widen the channel's prior if you have weak directional evidence.
- Report the model ROAS with a wide HDI and note the lack of calibration evidence.
- Recommend a future experiment in the next project phase.
